- Reverse-engineer my logical path for each move

- What's my solving approach? (scanning, constraint propagation, pattern hunting)
- Which techniques did I use? (naked singles, hidden singles, elimination)
- Spatial strategy: Row-first? Column-first? Box-first? Opportunistic?

- Explain Sudoku as a Constraint Satisfaction Problem (324 constraints)

- Show specific cells from MY board where I could apply new techniques

- Give me 3 targeted exercises addressing my weak patterns
- Ask Socratic questions: "Why did you choose this cell first?" "What made that number obvious?"

- Am I over-thinking (slow) or pattern-matching (fast)?
